Senior .NET Engineer

Northgate Labs · Manchester · Full-time · Posted 2 days ago

Apply now

About the role

Northgate Labs makes scheduling software for hospitals and regional transport operators. You'll join the Platform team, eight engineers who look after the .NET services behind every booking.

What you'll do

  • Design, build and run C# services on .NET 8 that handle millions of bookings a month
  • Shape API contracts with the product and mobile teams
  • Review code, pair with mid-level engineers and help them grow
  • Join a light on-call rota, one week in eight

What you'll bring

  • Five or more years building production systems in C# and .NET
  • Solid SQL Server skills, including query tuning
  • Experience with message-based systems such as Azure Service Bus or RabbitMQ
  • Clear, kind written communication

Nice to have

  • Azure Kubernetes Service or Terraform
  • A background in healthcare or transport software

About Northgate Labs

We're a team of 60 in Manchester, working hybrid with two office days a week. We care about calm releases, honest feedback and leaving on time.
